About the Manufacturer

About JJ & Company

Founded in 2016 in Jalgaon, Maharashtra. Direct manufacturer of Wallfin™ paint rollers (TM Application ID: 8027912) and direct importer of authentic Panama brushes for hardware dealers across western and central India.

Direct Manufacturing

Quality Painting Tools Engineered On-Site

JJ & Company (legal name J. J. & Company) is a paint accessories manufacturer founded in 2016 in Jalgaon, Maharashtra. The company was founded by Jafar Hanif Nagavadriya. We operate our own production floor and dispatch depot at Plot No-8, Dixit Wadi.

We manufacture Wallfin™ paint rollers under registered trademark ID 8027912. We also import authentic Panama paint brushes with 100% epoxy potting for shed-free performance. In addition, we supply thinners, wood polish, sandpaper, and putty pata for hardware shops.
