About the Manufacturer

About JJ & Company

Founded in 2016 in Jalgaon, Maharashtra. Direct manufacturer of Wallfin™ paint rollers (TM Application ID: 8027912) and direct importer of authentic Panama brushes for hardware dealers across western and central India.

Direct Manufacturing

Quality Painting Tools Engineered On-Site

JJ & Company (legal name J. J. & Company) is a dedicated paint accessories manufacturing enterprise established in 2016 in Jalgaon, Maharashtra, founded and directed by Jafar Hanif Nagavadriya. We operate our own production lines, assembly floor, and logistics dispatch depot at Plot No-8, Dixit Wadi.

Our core manufacturing specialty is in-house engineering of Wallfin™ paint rollers under our registered trademark (Application ID: 8027912). In addition, we directly import authentic Panama paint brushes from specialized brush manufacturing partners to guarantee 100% epoxy bristle potting with zero shedding. We also supply companion lines of thinner, polish, abrasives, and pata to provide hardware stockists with a comprehensive surface preparation catalog.
