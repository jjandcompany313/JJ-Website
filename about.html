Quality Protocol & Compliance

5-Step Manufacturing & Quality Protocol

Every roller cover, brush, and abrasive pack follows a zero-defect quality check before leaving our warehouse depot.

1

Raw Material Check

Inspection of virgin synthetic filaments, solvent-resistant polypropylene polymers, and stainless steel ferrules for tensile strength.

2

Precision Production

Automated thermo-fusion of roller naps, robotic bristle tufting, and epoxy adhesive resin potting for 100% shedding prevention.

3

QC Inspection

Optical inspection under magnifying lamps, pull-force gauge tests on bristles, solvent immersion tests, and diameter verification.

4

Protective Packing

Moisture-resistant poly wrap, hang-card retail sleeves, barcoded inner packs, and heavy-duty 5-ply export shipper cartons.

5

Fast Dispatch

Palletized inventory loaded onto scheduled factory delivery vans serving Jalgaon, Dhule, Nandurbar, Buldhana (MH) and Madhya Pradesh.
