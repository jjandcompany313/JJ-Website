Privacy Policy

How JJ & Company collects, protects, and handles business contact information from dealers and visitors.

Effective Date: October 2026 | Last Updated: October 24, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to JJ & Company (accessible at https://jjandcompany.net). We are a paint accessories manufacturer based at Plot No-8, Dixit Wadi, Jalgaon, Maharashtra 425001. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting any business or contact information you share with us through our website.

2. Information We Collect

We primarily operate as a business-to-business (B2B) manufacturer supplying hardware shops, paint stockists, and wholesalers. We collect information only when you voluntarily submit it via our contact or dealership enquiry forms, or reach out to us via WhatsApp or phone:

  • Contact Details: Your full name, phone number, and WhatsApp number.
  • Business Details: Hardware shop name, firm name, city, district, and state.
  • Order Requirements: Products of interest (Wall Fin rollers, Panama brushes, thinners, abrasives), estimated monthly volume, and inquiry messages.
  • Basic Technical Logs: Standard web analytics (such as pages visited and browser type via Google Analytics) to monitor website performance and improve user experience.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information submitted by you is used exclusively for legitimate business communication:

  • Responding to your pricing inquiries and dealership applications.
  • Sharing official PDF catalogues, wholesale rate sheets, and transport dispatch schedules via WhatsApp or email.
  • Processing bulk orders and arranging freight consignments to your city.

We never sell, rent, trade, or share your contact numbers or business data with third-party advertisers or telemarketers.

4. Communication via WhatsApp

Our website includes direct links to start chats on WhatsApp with our sales desk (+91 94227 75429). When you initiate a WhatsApp chat, your communication is also subject to WhatsApp's own privacy terms.

5. Data Security & Storage

We implement industry-standard HTTPS encryption across our entire domain to protect information in transit. Form submissions are transmitted directly to our secure internal inquiry ledger. Access to inquiry records is restricted strictly to authorized staff.

6. Your Rights

You may at any time request to review, update, or delete your contact details from our active dealer inquiry list by contacting us directly via phone or email.
