Frequently Asked Dealer Questions

Key details on becoming a retail stockist or wholesale dealer with JJ & Company.

1. How does a hardware shop or paint stockist become an authorized dealer?

You can apply online using the form on this page or message us directly on WhatsApp with your shop details and location. Our commercial desk will review your area and share distributor trade pricing and catalog.

2. Can we get sample packs to evaluate quality before bulk ordering?

Yes, we provide product sample packs (including Wall Fin roller handles, refills, and Panama paint brushes) for genuine retailers and stockists to test finish quality and bristle durability before placing volume orders.

3. What is the goods dispatch and transport process?

Goods are packed in heavy-duty master cartons at our Jalgaon factory and dispatched via established regional transport agencies serving Jalgaon, Dhule, Nandurbar, Buldhana, and Madhya Pradesh. Other regions are serviced via leading transport lines.

4. What is the Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?

Standard master carton quantities apply per model. For initial trial orders or regional dealership onboarding, we accommodate mixed-carton orders. Please contact our factory desk on WhatsApp (+91 94227 75429) for the latest MOQ schedule.
