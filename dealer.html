Frequently Asked Dealer Questions

Key operational details on becoming a retail stockist or wholesale dealer with JJ & Company.

How does a hardware shop or paint stockist become an authorized dealer?

You can apply online using the form on this page or message us directly on WhatsApp with your shop details and location. We manufacture Wall Fin® paint rollers (official registered brand) in Jalgaon and directly import Panama™ brushes from specialized overseas partner manufacturers, providing direct primary distributor rates.

Can we get sample packs to evaluate quality before bulk ordering?

Yes, we provide product sample packs (including Wall Fin® roller handles, refills, and imported Panama™ paint brushes) for genuine retailers and stockists to test finish quality and bristle durability before placing volume orders.

What is the goods dispatch and transport process?

Goods are packed in heavy-duty master cartons at our Jalgaon Depo and dispatched via established regional transport agencies serving Jalgaon, Dhule, Nandurbar, Buldhana, and Madhya Pradesh. Other regions are serviced via leading pan-India transport lines with full consignment docket tracking.

Can I place trial orders or mixed carton orders?

Yes. We welcome retail shops and new dealers with flexible order quantities and mixed carton assortments so you can test fast-moving models in your local market. Contact our depo desk on WhatsApp (+91 94227 75429) for immediate trial order assistance.
